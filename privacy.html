Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 26, 2026

What We Collect

When you use Stats Compass, files you upload are temporarily stored on our servers to enable data analysis. We do not require user accounts or collect personal information beyond what you choose to upload.

How We Use Your Data

Uploaded files are used solely to perform the analysis operations you request. Your data is not used for training AI models, and is not shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Sessions expire after 24 hours. When a session expires, all uploaded files and analysis results are permanently deleted.

Where Data Is Stored

Data is processed and stored on servers located in the United Kingdom (London).

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

Local Mode

When using Stats Compass in local mode (via pip install stats-compass-mcp), all data processing happens entirely on your machine. No data is sent to our servers.
