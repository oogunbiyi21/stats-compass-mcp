Four verdicts, not a wall of charts

A number on its own can't tell you whether to act. Stats Compass judges each one against what your own history says is normal for this time of year, and says so in plain words.

Expected

Within the range this time of year usually brings. Nothing to chase.

Real change

Outside the usual range, and too large to be chance. Worth acting on.

Noise

It moved, but by no more than week-to-week swings already do.

Not enough data yet

Too little history to judge, and we tell you how much more it needs.

What changed

A weekly report with a verdict on every headline figure: revenue, orders, average order value, margin, new and returning customers. "Nothing unusual happened" is a complete report.

What your promotions actually earned

Each discount's lift in revenue and contribution margin, measured against the sales that were coming anyway, with a range and a verdict. A busy week isn't a profitable one.

Real statistics for Shopify brands

Connect your Shopify store and Stats Compass reads your orders, products and customers. It never writes to your store. Your seasons, your promotions and your customers are judged against your own history, not an industry average.

Promotions, measured

Each promotion's lift in revenue, contribution margin and new customers, measured against the sales that were coming anyway, with the range each could fall in. The headline says what it needed to break even and what it brought, and the chart shows daily sales against what was expected without it.

The week, judged

Each headline figure with its verdict, and this week against the range this time of year usually brings. When something is worth a look, it leads the report: here, new customers falling five weeks in a row while revenue stays within its usual range.

Your seasons, forecast

Use Stats Compass with your AI assistant

Ask Claude about your store's orders in conversation. Set it up once, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Connect your store

    In the Stats Compass app in your Shopify admin, click Connect an AI assistant, on the overview or in Settings. Sign in when asked, with Google or email.

  2. 2

    Add Stats Compass to Claude

    On claude.ai, add a custom connector with:

    • Name: Stats Compass
    • URL: https://mcp.statscompass.io/mcp
  3. 3

    Sign in the same way

    When Claude asks you to sign in, use the same sign-in as in step 1, for example the same Google account. A different sign-in is a different account, and won't see your store.

  4. 4

    Ask about your store

    Start with "Use stats compass to…", for example:

    • "Use stats compass to load my store's orders and chart weekly revenue for the past year."
    • "Use stats compass to find which discount codes brought in the most revenue last quarter."
    • "Use stats compass to compare last week's revenue with the same week last year."

Using ChatGPT, VS Code or Cursor? Add Stats Compass with the install options, then sign in the same way as in step 1.

Questions

What happens after early access?

The app is free during early access. If we introduce paid plans, we'll tell you before anything changes, and you choose whether to stay.

Does it change my store?

No. Stats Compass reads your orders, products and customers. It never writes to your store.

How much history does it need?

About 13 months of orders, so each week and each promotion can be compared with the same time last year. With less, it says "Not enough data yet" for what it can't judge, and how much more it needs.
