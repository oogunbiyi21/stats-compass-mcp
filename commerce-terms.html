Part 1: Using the app

1. Agreeing to these terms

2. What the app does

The app reads your store's orders, products and customers through Shopify, with read-only access: it cannot change anything in your store. It works out your store's figures, judges each one against your store's own history, and shows the results in the app and in a weekly report email. If you choose, you can also connect an AI assistant and ask it about your store.

You decide what to do with the app's figures. They are statistical estimates from your store's history, shown with their ranges. They are not financial, tax or legal advice.

Part 2: Data processing

12. Roles and purpose

For the personal data in your store's information, you are the controller and we are your processor. We process it under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), and under the EU GDPR where that applies to you.

We process it only to:

  • work out your store's figures, judge them against your store's own history, and show them in the app and in the weekly report;
  • answer your AI assistant's questions about your store, if you connect one, and only when you ask.

We do not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or combine it with other stores' data. These terms, your settings in the app, and the features you choose to use are your instructions to us. If we think an instruction breaks data protection law, we will tell you.

We process it for as long as the app is installed, and then until it is deleted as section 16 describes.

13. What we process

About your customers:

  • Shopify's identifier for each customer, how many orders they have placed, and when their customer record was created;
  • their orders: dates, amounts and currency, products and quantities, discounts, discount codes and the names of automatic discounts, refunds, the payment gateway, order tags and sales channel, and whether an order was a test.

We do not request or store your customers' names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses. We do not process special category data.

About your staff: the Shopify staff account that changes a setting, and the email addresses you enter to receive the weekly report.

Not personal data, listed for completeness: your products, their variants and their costs, and your store's settings.

14. Keeping it safe

  • In transit: information moving between Shopify, the app and you is encrypted (TLS).
  • At rest: the database, stored files and backups are stored encrypted.
  • Your store's access key: the key the app uses to read your store is stored encrypted.
  • Separation: each store's data is kept apart from every other store's by the database itself (row-level security), and each part of the app has only the database access it needs.
  • Read-only: the app cannot change anything in your store.
  • People: only the people who run the service can reach your data, and they are bound to keep it confidential.

16. Retention and deletion

  • While the app is installed, we keep your store's data so the figures can be calculated and checked.
  • After you uninstall, we delete everything we hold for your store, both data and files, including the charts in past report emails, 30 days after you uninstall, unless you reinstall first. Shopify also asks us to delete it, about two days after you uninstall.
  • Backups are kept for 30 days, so deleted data leaves our backups within a further 30 days.
  • Sooner: you can ask us to delete your store's data from Settings → Your data, or by email.

17. Helping you with your obligations

Your customers' requests. When one of your customers asks Shopify for their data, or for it to be deleted, Shopify passes the request to us.

  • A data request: we hold that customer's Shopify identifier, the date of their first order, when their Shopify customer record was created, how many orders they have placed, and their orders, all of which you can also see in Shopify. Ask us and we will send you what we hold.
  • A deletion request: we replace their Shopify identifier with a random one everywhere we keep it. Their orders stay, so your store's figures don't change, but we no longer hold Shopify's identifier for them. Our backups still hold it for up to 30 days.

We will also give you reasonable help with data protection impact assessments, and with questions from a data protection authority, about the app's processing.

Breaches. If a personal data breach affects your store's data, we will tell you without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. We will say what happened, what data is affected, what it may mean, and what we are doing about it, and we will keep you updated as we learn more.

Showing we comply. We will give you the information you reasonably need to show that we meet these terms. You may audit our compliance once a year, on 30 days' written notice, at your own cost, without disrupting the service, and under confidentiality. Where we can, we will answer an audit with documents instead.
